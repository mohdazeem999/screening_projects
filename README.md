# 🧠 LearnIQ

### AI-Powered Classroom Learning Intelligence & Personalized Improvement Platform

> **Assess → Understand → Improve → Measure → Recognize → Reward**

LearnIQ is an AI-powered educational platform designed for **Classes 5–12** that transforms traditional assessments into actionable learning intelligence.

Instead of simply showing students their marks, LearnIQ analyzes assessment performance to identify **learning gaps, recurring mistakes, areas of improvement, and classroom-level weaknesses**.

The platform then uses these insights to provide targeted practice, track improvement, recognize progress, and motivate students through a controlled points and rewards system.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Traditional assessment systems primarily answer:

> **"What did the student score?"**

But teachers also need answers to:

* What concepts does the student struggle with?
* Why are students making repeated mistakes?
* Which topics are difficult for the entire class?
* What should the teacher teach or revise next?
* Is a student's performance actually improving?
* How can students who improve significantly be recognized?
* How can continuous learning be encouraged?

LearnIQ aims to address these problems by converting assessment data into **actionable learning insights**.

---

## 🔍 Learning Gap Detection

Instead of only displaying a percentage, the system creates a learning profile.

Example:

| Concept          | Status            |
| ---------------- | ----------------- |
| Fractions        | Strong            |
| Algebra          | Moderate          |
| Linear Equations | Needs Improvement |
| Geometry         | Strong            |
| Data Handling    | Needs Practice    |

---

## 🧠 Mistake Intelligence

The system can analyze incorrect responses and identify possible categories such as:

* Conceptual error
* Calculation error
* Misinterpretation
* Incorrect method
* Incomplete understanding

These classifications are intended as learning insights rather than definitive diagnoses.

# 🏆 Smart Ranking & Recognition

LearnIQ is designed to recognize more than just the highest score.

Possible categories include:

### 🥇 Overall Performance

Recognition based on configured assessment performance.

### 📈 Most Improved

Students showing significant improvement from previous assessments.

### 🔥 Consistency

Students maintaining regular learning activity.

### 🧠 Concept Mastery

Students demonstrating progress in targeted concepts.

### 🎯 Challenge Achievement

Students completing learning challenges.

### 📚 Subject Progress

Recognition for progress within a particular subject.

This allows students with different strengths to receive meaningful recognition.

---

# ⭐ Learning Points

Students can earn points for meaningful learning activities such as:

* Completing assessments
* Completing targeted practice
* Improving previous performance
* Completing challenges
* Demonstrating concept mastery
* Completing revision activities
* Maintaining learning consistency

The point rules can be configured by the school/teacher.

# 🎁 Reward System

Students can redeem eligible points for **school-approved educational rewards**.

Examples:

* ✏️ Pens
* 📓 Notebooks
* 📚 Books
* 🧴 Water bottles
* 🎒 Study kits
* 📅 Study planners
* 📖 Educational materials

### Redemption Flow

```text
Earn Points
     ↓
Open Rewards
     ↓
Select Reward
     ↓
Check Required Points
     ↓
Submit Redemption
     ↓
Teacher/Admin Approval
     ↓
Reward Fulfilment
```

The reward system is intended to be controlled by the school or authorized teacher/admin.

```text
Assessment
     ↓
Learning Intelligence
     ↓
Personalized Intervention
     ↓
Improvement Measurement
     ↓
Recognition
     ↓
Reward
     ↓
Further Learning
```
